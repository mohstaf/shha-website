SHHA begins engagement with the World Federation of Hemophilia

2026Partnerships

In 2026, SHHA began engagement with the World Federation of Hemophilia (WFH) and with regional and international partners working on bleeding disorders.

Why it matters

Somalia has no haemophilia treatment centre and no clotting factor concentrate in the public health system. Many boys are first noticed only after heavy bleeding, often after circumcision.

What SHHA hopes to achieve

SHHA sees this engagement as a first step towards:

  • training for doctors, nurses and laboratory staff in diagnosis and comprehensive care
  • better data, through the confidential national patient register
  • donated clotting factor concentrates, used safely under qualified clinicians

Health workers can already use free WFH guidelines and e-courses. Find the links on our For health workers page.

What comes next

We will report progress here as it happens. Organisations are named as partners only once they have agreed in writing.

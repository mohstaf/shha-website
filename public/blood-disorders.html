Blood disorders

These conditions are passed from parents to children through their genes. They are lifelong, but with early diagnosis and the right care, children can live full lives.

The conditions we work on

Haemophilia The blood does not clot properly because a clotting factor is missing or low. It mainly affects boys and men. Von Willebrand disease The most common inherited bleeding disorder, with rare factor deficiencies. It affects men and women equally. Sickle cell disease Red blood cells become sickle-shaped and block blood flow, causing pain and anaemia. Thalassaemia The body makes too little normal haemoglobin. Severe forms need regular transfusions. Inheritance and carrier testing How these conditions pass from parents to children, and how a blood test shows whether you are a carrier.

Worried about yourself or your child? Contact SHHA. We will tell you where to get tested and treated, and register you so you are not forgotten.

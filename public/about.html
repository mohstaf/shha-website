Who we are

The Somali Haemophilia and Haemoglobinopathies Association (SHHA) is a national, non-profit, volunteer-led organisation based in Mogadishu. It is dedicated to improving the lives of people living with haemophilia and other inherited bleeding disorders, sickle cell disease and thalassaemia across Somalia.

SHHA brings together patients, families, clinicians and laboratory professionals with one shared purpose: to make sure no Somali child or adult with an inherited blood disorder goes undiagnosed, untreated or unsupported.

Vision, mission and values

Vision

A Somalia where every person with an inherited blood disorder is diagnosed early, treated safely and empowered to live a full and healthy life.

Mission

To improve diagnosis, treatment and quality of life for people with inherited blood disorders through patient advocacy, awareness, education, partnership and support for the strengthening of the national health system.

Core values

Patient-centred

The needs and voices of patients and families guide everything we do.

Equity

Care for everyone, in every region, regardless of gender, age, clan or income.

Integrity and transparency

Honest use of resources and open reporting to members and partners.

Partnership

Working with government, clinicians and national and international partners.

Evidence

Decisions guided by data, international guidelines and patient experience.

Our objectives

  1. Identify and register people with inherited blood disorders across Somalia.
  2. Advocate with government and partners for access to diagnosis, treatment and comprehensive care.
  3. Raise public and professional awareness of haemophilia, bleeding disorders and haemoglobinopathies.
  4. Educate patients, families and caregivers, and build the capacity of health workers.
  5. Facilitate access to essential treatment, including donated clotting factor concentrates.
  6. Promote research and data that guide policy and services.
  7. Mobilise resources to sustain the Association's work.

Our programmes

Our story

SHHA was founded in Mogadishu in 2026 by patients, families and health professionals who saw children with bleeding disorders and haemoglobinopathies going undiagnosed and untreated.

SHHA is a new national association with a mandate for both bleeding disorders and haemoglobinopathies. Our first task is to build a confidential national register, so that every family can be reached.

Milestones

  1. 2026Association founded in Mogadishu
  2. 2026Mandate broadened to haemoglobinopathies, as SHHA
  3. 2026Registration initiated with the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation
  4. 2026Engagement began with the World Federation of Hemophilia and regional and international partners

Board and governance

SHHA is governed by an elected Board and a General Assembly of members, and is advised by a Medical Advisory Committee of haematologists, paediatricians and laboratory professionals. The Association is run by volunteers and works closely with clinicians across Somalia.

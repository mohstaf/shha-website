SHHA is run by volunteers. Your time and skills help Somali families get diagnosed and treated.

Partner with us

SHHA offers partners access to patients and families, with their consent, local knowledge and a national network of clinicians and laboratory professionals. Contact the President, Abdirasak Sharif Ali, at info@haemophilia-somalia.org.

Email the President
Somali Haemophilia and Haemoglobinopathies Association

A national, non-profit, volunteer-led organisation based in Mogadishu, for people living with inherited blood disorders.

Registration with the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation is in progress.

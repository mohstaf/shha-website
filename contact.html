Common questions

Is help from SHHA free?

Yes. Advice, information and registration with SHHA are free.

Can I talk to you in Somali?

Yes. You can message or call us in Somali or English.

Will my information be kept private?

Yes. We use your details only to help you and to plan services. They are never published. Read our privacy policy.

I am a health worker. How do I refer a patient?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or email with the patient’s details and their consent. See For health workers.

How can my organisation work with SHHA?

Email us or read how to partner with us.

Somali Haemophilia and Haemoglobinopathies Association

A national, non-profit, volunteer-led organisation based in Mogadishu, for people living with inherited blood disorders.

Registration with the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation is in progress.

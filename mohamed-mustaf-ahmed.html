Mohamed Mustaf Ahmed

MD, MSc

Clinical Data Scientist, SHHA

About

A medical doctor and data scientist who supports SHHA’s research and development.

His work uses machine learning, disease modelling and surveillance data to strengthen health systems in Somalia and across Africa. At SHHA he helps build the patient registry, data systems and research that will guide services for people with inherited blood disorders.

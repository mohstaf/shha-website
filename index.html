Why SHHA exists

Care for inherited blood disorders in Somalia is only beginning.

  • There is no haemophilia treatment centre anywhere in Somalia.
  • There is no clotting factor in the public health system.
  • There is no newborn screening for sickle cell disease or thalassaemia.

SHHA was founded in Mogadishu in 2026 to change this.

About SHHA

Who we are

SHHA brings together patients, families, clinicians and laboratory professionals. One purpose: no Somali child or adult with an inherited blood disorder goes undiagnosed, untreated or unsupported.

Know the warning signs

Inherited and lifelong. With early diagnosis and the right care, children grow up to live full lives.

All blood disorders
01Haemophilia 02Sickle cell disease 03Thalassaemia

01Haemophilia

The blood does not clot properly. Many boys are first noticed after circumcision.

Watch for

  1. Bleeding that will not stop after a cut or circumcision
  2. Large bruises from small bumps
  3. Swollen, painful joints
About haemophilia

02Sickle cell disease

Red blood cells turn stiff and sickle-shaped, and block blood flow.

Watch for

  1. Sudden, severe pain
  2. Swollen hands and feet in babies
  3. Frequent infections and fever
About sickle cell disease

03Thalassaemia

The body makes too little haemoglobin. Severe forms need regular blood transfusions.

Watch for

  1. Pale skin and tiredness
  2. Poor growth and feeding
  3. A swollen belly
About thalassaemia

Our programmes

Six areas of work, from the first national register to better care.

All programmes
Patient registryA confidential national register of people with inherited blood disorders. Awareness and educationFamily education days and clear information in Somali. Capacity buildingTraining doctors, nurses and laboratory staff in diagnosis and care. Access to treatmentSeeking donated clotting factor, used safely under qualified clinicians. HaemoglobinopathiesEvidence and care pathways for sickle cell disease and thalassaemia. AdvocacyMaking inherited blood disorders a national health priority.

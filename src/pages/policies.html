Privacy policy

What we collect

The registration form asks for name, age, sex, region, diagnosis, phone number and who is filling it in. The contact form asks for name, email or phone, and your message.

Why

To contact you, tell you where to get tested and treated, plan services and request donated treatment. We report only anonymous totals, such as the number of people registered in each region.

Who sees it

A small number of named SHHA volunteers. We never sell or publish your details, and we share them with a health worker only with your permission. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also covered by WhatsApp's own policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them, and we delete them if you ask us to.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, email info@shha.so or message us on WhatsApp. We will reply as soon as we can.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Medical disclaimer

This website gives general information, not medical advice. Always follow the advice of a qualified health worker who knows the patient.

In an emergency, such as heavy bleeding, a head injury or a severe pain crisis, go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Consent for photos and stories

We use a photo or story only with written consent from the person, or from a parent or carer for a child. We do not name children or show a diagnosis next to a recognisable photo without that consent.

You can withdraw consent at any time, and we will remove the material from the website as soon as we can.

Funding and conflicts of interest

SHHA is a new volunteer-led association. We will publish our funders here, and in our annual report, as funding is received.

Support from industry

  • SHHA decides its own programmes and messages.
  • We do not promote any company's products.
  • We declare all industry support publicly, with amounts, in our annual report.
  • Donated treatment is used only under qualified clinicians.
  • Board members declare any conflict of interest and take no part in related decisions.

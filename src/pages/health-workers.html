Refer a patient

  1. Ask for consentAsk the patient or family if they agree to be contacted by SHHA.
  2. Send the detailsTheir age, region, phone number, symptoms and any test results, with your name and facility.
  3. We take it from thereWe contact the family, help with testing and register them.

Do not send photos of the patient. You can also use the registration form with the family's agreement.

Suspected bleeding disorder before circumcision or surgery

Before circumcision, tooth extraction or surgery, ask about bleeding in the family and long bleeding after earlier cuts or injections.

If you suspect a bleeding disorder, delay any planned procedure, request a CBC and coagulation screen, and seek specialist advice. A normal screen does not rule out mild haemophilia or von Willebrand disease. Avoid intramuscular injections, aspirin and ibuprofen.

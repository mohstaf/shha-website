Abdirasak Sharif Ali

BSc, MSc

President, SHHA

About

A haematologist and researcher working on anaemia, inherited blood disorders and blood transfusion in Somalia.

His work joins laboratory studies with analysis of national survey data, including a national meta-analysis of anaemia prevalence. He is a Senior Lecturer and Researcher in Haematology at SIMAD University, Mogadishu, and a Senior Research Associate at the SIMAD Institute for Global Health.

SHHA is founded in Mogadishu

2026Organisation

In 2026, patients, families and health professionals in Mogadishu founded the Somali Haemophilia and Haemoglobinopathies Association (SHHA). They had seen too many children with bleeding disorders and haemoglobinopathies go undiagnosed and untreated.

SHHA is a national, non-profit, volunteer-led association with a mandate for both bleeding disorders and haemoglobinopathies, including sickle cell disease and thalassaemia.

Why it matters

Somalia has no haemophilia treatment centre and no clotting factor concentrate in the public health system. There is no newborn screening and no national data for sickle cell disease or thalassaemia.

SHHA's first step is a confidential national register, so that families can be reached with testing and treatment.

How SHHA is run

SHHA is governed by an elected Board and a General Assembly of members, and advised by a Medical Advisory Committee of haematologists, paediatricians and laboratory professionals. Its President is Abdirasak Sharif Ali.

Registration with the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation has been initiated.

Get help

If you or your child may have one of these conditions, message us on WhatsApp, 24 hours or register with SHHA.
